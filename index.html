About

I am an academic researcher specializing in financial economics and quantitative asset pricing. My research focuses on understanding risk dynamics, stochastic discount factor models, production-based asset pricing, and empirical market microstructure.

Research Interests

  • Empirical & Theoretical Asset Pricing: Stochastic Discount Factor (SDF) modeling, cross-sectional return predictability, and macro-finance linkages.
  • Production-Based Asset Pricing: Firm investment dynamics, revenue horizon structures, and corporate capital constraints.
  • Quantitative Portfolio Theory: Downside risk metrics, tail risk hedging, and nonlinear factor exposures.
